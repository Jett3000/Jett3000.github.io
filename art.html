Art

Jett Pavlica is a computational artist who draws with machines: pieces begin as abstract code and become realized through play, iteration, and the ritual choreography of a pen plotter delivering ink to paper. Their practice invites both structure and surprise, letting digital systems meet the chaos of pigments and fibers. Queerness, southern ecologies, and a deep love of math shape a visual language that is constantly asking questions about the world around them, while imagining harmony, serenity, and healing.
